This Privacy Policy explains what Find My Best Shades ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, why, and the choices you have. We run this site from Vietnam and serve customers mainly in the United States. Questions or requests: findmybestshades@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your photo. The selfie you upload for a color reading. Face detection and color sampling run in your browser, and this photo check happens before you pay. After you pay, a copy is also sent, securely, to an AI service to help interpret the reading (see "Who we share with").
  • Color measurements from your photo. Numeric color values for your skin, iris, eye whites and eyebrows, plus the resulting season and scores. We do not store your photo, a face template, or any data used to identify who you are.
  • Your name (optional). The first name you type in, used only to greet you. It stays in your browser.
  • Your email and order details. If you pay, PayPal gives us your order confirmation and email so we can let you restore purchased checks and help with any problem with your order. We never see or store your card details.
  • Approximate location. Your city, region and country, estimated from your IP address, to understand where our visitors are.
  • Feedback. Star ratings, comments, and the short reason you pick if you leave without downloading your results.
  • Data stored in your browser. Your remaining checks, name, scan-attempt count and feedback history are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

How we use it

To produce your color reading and shade matches, take payment, restore your purchases, help with any problem with your order, fix problems, improve the accuracy of our matching, and respond to you. We do not sell your personal information, do not share it for advertising, and do not use your photo to train any model.

Who we share with

  • PayPal processes payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) receives your photo, sent through our server, to help interpret your reading. It is used to return your result.
  • Google hosts the spreadsheet where we log measurements, feedback and order events, serves our fonts, and hosts the face-detection model your browser downloads.
  • jsDelivr serves the face-detection code your browser loads.
  • ipwho.is looks up an approximate location from your IP address.
  • Netlify hosts this website.

These providers process data on our behalf or under their own policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Face and biometric information

Analysis of your photo involves detecting facial landmarks in order to find where your skin, eyes and eyebrows are. We use this only to sample colors. We do not use it to recognize or identify you, we do not create or keep a face template, and landmark data is discarded when your reading finishes. By uploading a photo you consent to this analysis.

How long we keep things

We do not keep your photo. Numeric measurements, feedback and order records are kept for as long as we need them to run and improve the service, then deleted. You can ask us to delete your records at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Residents of California and certain other US states have specific legal rights over their personal information; email us and we will honor valid requests. You can also clear your browser's site data, or simply not upload a photo.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. PayPal may set its own cookies while its payment buttons load. Our own browser storage is described above.

Children

This service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

International use

Your information is processed in the United States and in Vietnam, where we operate, and wherever our providers run their systems.
