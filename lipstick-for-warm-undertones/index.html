Questions

Can warm undertones wear pink?

Yes, choose warm pinks: peachy pink, coral pink or salmon, rather than icy or bluish pinks.

What red suits warm undertones?

Orange-reds and brick reds. Look for a red that leans toward tomato rather than toward berry.

What nude suits warm skin?

A peachy, caramel or warm brown nude close to your natural lip color.
