About Us

Your colors, measured — not guessed

Find My Best Shades is a personal color analysis tool that finds the color season and lipstick shades that harmonize with your natural coloring.

We believe every woman already holds her own true beauty. Our goal is to help you see which colors work with it.

What we do

Most color quizzes ask you to guess your own undertone. We don't. You upload a clear selfie, and we measure the real colors of your skin, iris, eye whites and eyebrows directly from the pixels. From those measurements we work out your undertone, depth, contrast and intensity, match them to one of 16 color seasons, and rank lipstick shades from MAC, Revlon, L'Oréal Paris and Maybelline by how closely each one fits you.

How our matching works

We detect your face in your browser, sample the true colors of each feature, and convert them into a color space built around how the eye sees color. That lets us measure warmth, lightness and color intensity as numbers instead of impressions. We keep refining the matching against real customer photos to make it as accurate as we can across different lighting and cameras.

What we promise

Honest picks

Shades are ranked only by how well they fit your measured coloring. Our buy links go to each brand's official website and we don't currently earn a commission from them.

Your photo stays yours

We use your photo only to measure colors. We don't keep it, and we don't try to identify you. Read the details in our Privacy Policy.

No paying for a bad photo

We check your photo for lighting and a clear face before you pay, so you're only charged once we know we can read it. See our Refund Policy.

Who's behind it

Find My Best Shades is created and run independently by a Vietnamese beauty content creator who builds AI-powered color tools. It's a small independent project, so if something looks off or you have an idea, we read every message: findmybestshades@gmail.com.

We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the brands we match. They are named only so you can find the products.
